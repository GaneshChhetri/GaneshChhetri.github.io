What I work on

Most of what I build is the software an organisation actually runs on: registration and licensing workflows, approval hierarchies, permission models, reporting and payments. I take it from the first requirements conversation through the schema, the services and the screens, into production — and then stay with it, because the interesting problems arrive after go-live.

For the past two years that has meant leading delivery. Before it, keeping live systems healthy under real users, and building a national health platform on AWS. A thread runs through nearly all of it: I am the person who has wired Bhutan NDI, the national digital identity, into system after system. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering specialising in cloud technology and information security, and I am comfortable owning the parts most people hand off — the database, the deployment, and the security findings.
